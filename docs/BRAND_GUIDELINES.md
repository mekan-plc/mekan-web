## Before you begin

This guide describes how Mekan looks, sounds and presents itself: on the website, in a proposal, on a training certificate, on a slide in front of a room of farm managers. It is written for everyone who makes something that carries the Mekan name. That includes the Mekan team, freelance consultants and trainers, designers, printers, developers and anyone else we invite to work with us.

It is not a rulebook for its own sake. Every decision here exists so that a farm owner in Ziway, a buyer in Rotterdam and a programme officer in Brussels all meet the same Mekan: careful, capable and easy to trust.

When something is not covered here, choose the simpler option, and the one that looks most like the rest of the brand.

## 1. Our story

In Ethiopia, before a door is hung, a carpenter sets the *mekan*: the frame.

It is the part of the doorway nobody stops to admire, and the part everything depends on. It has to stand square and true. It carries the weight. And because it holds, the door can open, close and open again for years without sagging or sticking.

We took our name from that frame because it describes our work exactly.

Before an Ethiopian farm can sell to a supermarket in Europe, before a cooperative can carry a certification mark on its boxes, before a processor can stand behind every product that leaves its gate, something has to be set square first. Quality systems. Food safety controls. Records that hold up in an audit. People who are trained, confident and able to keep the standard on an ordinary Tuesday, not just on audit day. That is what we build.

We do not stand in the doorway. We build the frame that lets the door open, and we make sure it stays open.

Look at our logo and you will see the same idea: two frames, one overlapping the other. One is the Ethiopian producer, rooted in the land and in generations of knowledge. The other is the global market, with its standards, its buyers and its promise. Mekan works in the space where they meet.

> **Our brand idea:** Mekan builds the frame. Markets open.
>
> This line guides how we think and how we present ourselves. It is an internal compass, not a slogan to print everywhere.

## 4. Our name

How we write the name matters, because it is the first thing people see.

| Use | Name | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Full legal name** | Mekan Quality Management & Training PLC | Contracts, invoices, letterheads, certificates, the website footer and legal pages, the first mention in formal documents |
| **Short name** | Mekan | Everyday use once the full name has appeared: "Mekan's trainers", "Contact Mekan" |
| **Descriptive short form** | Mekan Consultancy | Allowed where space is tight or where "consultancy" helps a first-time reader understand what we do, such as the website header |

**Rules**

- Always write **Mekan** with a capital M and lower-case "ekan". Never MEKAN in running text, never "Mekkan" or "Mekane".
- Use **&** in the full legal name, exactly as registered. In running sentences, write "quality management and training".
- **PLC** is always in capitals and only appears with the full legal name.
- Within one document or one web page, stay consistent. Do not switch between "Mekan PLC", "Mekan Consultancy" and "Mekan" in the same paragraph.

**Descriptor:** *Quality management, compliance, training.* This is the line that sits with our logo. It tells a newcomer exactly what we do in three words.

## 5. Our logo

The Mekan logo is two overlapping frames, one green and one blue, with the Mekan wordmark. It is our most recognisable asset, and it stays exactly as it is.

### Versions

| Version | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Full logo** | `logo.png` | The frames with the full company name and descriptor. Documents, letterheads, proposals, footers. |
| **Mark** | `logo1.2.png` | The frames with the Mekan wordmark. Website header, favicon, social media profile picture, small spaces. |

### Backgrounds

- The logo is designed for **white or Paper** backgrounds. That is where it always looks its best.
- On **navy, a dark colour or a photograph**, place the logo on a white panel with comfortable space around it. Never put the colour logo directly on a busy image or a mid-tone colour where the frames get lost.

### Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** leave empty space on every side of the logo at least half the height of the frames. Text, edges and other logos stay outside it.
- **Minimum size:** the mark is never smaller than **32 px** tall on screen or **10 mm** in print. The full logo is never narrower than **180 px** on screen or **45 mm** in print. Below that, use the mark.

### Please never

- Stretch, squash, tilt or rotate the logo.
- Change, swap or adjust its colours, including recolouring it to match the brand palette.
- Add shadows, outlines, glows, gradients or 3D effects.
- Retype the wordmark or the Ge'ez letters in any font, or trace the logo from a screenshot.
- Separate the frames from the wordmark, or use one frame alone as a decoration.
- Place it inside another shape or crowd it with other graphics.

**Files:** always use the official files from the Mekan brand folder. If a print supplier needs a vector file (SVG, PDF or EPS), ask Mekan for the master artwork. Never let a supplier redraw it.

> **A note on logo colours.** The green and blue inside the logo are part of the artwork and stay unchanged. The brand palette in section 6 is the palette for everything around the logo: the website, documents and presentations. The two are related on purpose. They are not meant to be identical.

### Balance

Most of what we make should feel light and open. As a guide for screens and documents:

- **About 65%** Paper and White
- **About 15%** Black text
- **About 12%** Mekan Navy
- **About 6%** Mekan Green
- **About 2%** Sky and Sprout together

Our photographs bring the rest of the colour: the greens of the fields, the reds and yellows of the produce. That is why the palette itself stays disciplined, and why we use no orange, gold or other extra colours.

### Combinations that work

Every pairing below meets or exceeds the WCAG AA contrast standard for normal text (4.5 : 1).

| Text | On | Contrast |

| Mekan Navy | White / Paper | 13.8 : 1 / 13.2 : 1 |
| Black | White / Paper | 18.9 : 1 / 18.1 : 1 |
| Grey | White / Paper | 6.5 : 1 / 6.2 : 1 |
| Mekan Green | White / Paper | 5.1 : 1 / 4.8 : 1 |
| White | Mekan Navy | 13.8 : 1 |

### Combinations to avoid

- **Mekan Green on Navy, or Navy on Green.** They are too close in depth and blur together.
- **Navy on dark backgrounds.** It disappears. Use Sky instead.
- **Mekan Green as text in dark mode.** Use Sprout instead.
- **Sky or Sprout on white or Paper.** They are too light to read. They belong on navy and dark surfaces only.
- **Text on photographs** without a solid or strongly darkened area behind it.

### Colour in print

Screens and paper show colour differently. The HEX values above are the master values. Before the first print run of anything important (signage, banners, business cards, certificates), Mekan's printer prepares the CMYK and Pantone matches for Mekan Navy and Mekan Green from a printed proof, and Mekan approves the proof in person. Those approved values are then recorded in the brand folder, so every printer works from the same numbers.

## 7. Typography

Two typefaces, each with a clear job.

**Source Serif 4** gives our headings their authority. It has the calm, considered feel of a well-made report or a respected journal, echoes the serif wordmark in our logo, and tells the reader at once that this is serious, expert work.

**Inter** carries everything else. It was designed for screens, stays clear at small sizes, and handles technical detail, tables and forms without effort.

Both are free from Google Fonts.

| Typeface | Used for | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| **Source Serif 4** | Page titles, headings, large numbers, pull quotes | SemiBold (600); Medium (500) for very large hero headlines |
| **Inter** | Body text, navigation, buttons, forms, labels, captions, tables | Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600) |
| **Noto Serif Ethiopic / Noto Sans Ethiopic** | Amharic text, when needed, paired with Source Serif 4 and Inter respectively | Matching weights |

**Fallbacks.** Where the brand fonts are not installed, for example in everyday Word or PowerPoint files, use **Georgia** for headings and **Arial** for body text. One document never mixes brand fonts with fallbacks.

| Section heading | Source Serif 4 SemiBold | 16 pt | Mekan Navy |
| Sub-heading | Inter SemiBold | 11.5 pt | Black |
| Body text | Inter Regular | 10.5 pt | Black |
| Tables and captions | Inter Regular | 8.5 pt | Grey |

### Habits of good Mekan typography

- **Sentence case for headings.** "Global standards and certifications", not "Global Standards And Certifications". Proper names and standard names keep their own capitals (GLOBALG.A.P, BRCGS, HACCP).
- **Left-aligned text.** Never justified. Centre only short headlines and calls to action.
- **Keep lines readable.** Around 60–75 characters per line for body text on the web.
- **Bold only what must not be missed.** If everything is bold, nothing is.
- **Write standards correctly.** GLOBALG.A.P, BRCGS, IFS, HACCP, FSMS 22000, GRASP, SMETA, LEAF, Tesco Nurture.

## 8. The frame: our signature graphic element

Our name gives us a graphic device no one else can claim: **the frame**.

Taken from the logo, the frame is a thin rectangular outline: a 1–2 px line in Mekan Navy, Mekan Green or Sky. It is used quietly, the way a good carpenter's work is quiet.

**Ways to use it**

- **The offset frame.** An outline frame set slightly behind and offset from a photograph, echoing the two overlapping frames of the logo. Our most distinctive move, used once or twice per page at most.
- **Corner marks.** Short L-shaped lines at the corners of an image, a key number or a quote, suggesting a frame without drawing all of it.
- **The rule.** A thin horizontal line under a heading, across the foot of a letterhead or along the edge of a cover.
- **The grid.** Faint frame lines on navy backgrounds (at 5–8% opacity), like the lines of a technical drawing.

**Ways not to use it**

- Never as a heavy border, a thick box or a filled shape.
- Never more than one frame treatment in the same view.
- Never with drop shadows, gradients or glows.
- Never as a repeated pattern or wallpaper.

## 9. Layout, shape and space

A Mekan layout should feel like a well-organised farm: everything has its place, and there is room to move.

- **Space is part of the design.** Generous margins and section spacing make us look confident. A crowded page looks anxious.
- **A clear grid.** Content aligns to a simple column grid. On the website, content sits within a maximum width of about 1200 px.
- **One idea per section.** Each section has one job: introduce, prove, explain or invite.
- **Corners are soft but not round.** Cards and images: 16 px radius. Buttons and form fields: 10 px. Small badges and tags: fully rounded.
- **Shadows are soft and rare.** A light shadow lifts a card on hover. It never sits under everything.
- **Icons** are simple line icons with a consistent 1.75–2 px stroke, in a single brand colour. Use them to help people find or understand something, never as decoration.
- **Charts** use Mekan Navy first, then Mekan Green, then Sky, then the tints. Labels and axes in Grey. No 3D, no shadows, no rainbow palettes.

### Website components

| Component | Light mode | Dark mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Page** | Paper `#FAFAF8` | Dark page `#0B0B0B` |
| **Card** | White `#FFFFFF`, 1 px Line `#E6E6E1`, 16 px radius | Dark card `#161616`, 1 px Dark line `#262626`, 16 px radius |
| **Primary button** | Navy fill, white text | Navy fill, white text (with a Sky outline on hover) |
| **Secondary button** | Mekan Green fill, white text, or a 1 px outline | Sprout outline, light text |
| **Button on navy** | White fill, Navy text | White fill, Navy text |
| **Link** | Mekan Green, underline on hover | Sprout, underline on hover |
| **Form field** | White, 1 px Line, 10 px radius, Navy focus ring | Dark card, 1 px Dark line, Sky focus ring |
| **Brand band** (header, footer, call to action) | Mekan Navy | Mekan Navy |

### Motion

Movement on the website should feel like a door swinging smoothly on a good frame: steady, never showy.

- Hover and press feedback: **150–250 ms**, ease-out.
- Content appearing on scroll: a gentle fade and short rise, **600–800 ms**, played once.
- Nothing bounces, spins or flashes.
- When a visitor's device asks for reduced motion, animations are switched off.

## 10. Photography

Our photographs are real. Real farms, real produce, real people at work, from projects Mekan has been part of. That authenticity is one of the most persuasive things our brand has, and we protect it.

**What we show**

- Farms, fields and greenhouses where our clients work.
- Produce at its best: harvested, graded, packed and ready to travel.
- Pack houses, cold rooms and processing lines that meet the standard.
- People: trainers in a session, teams in the field, farm workers with their skills and dignity visible.
- Mekan's own team at work alongside clients.

**How we choose and use photographs**

- **Real over perfect.** A genuine photo from a real project is always better than a polished stock image. We do not use stock photography to represent Mekan's work.
- **One strong image beats many weak ones.** Choose the clearest, best-lit photo for the most important places, such as the home page hero.
- **Crop with care.** Keep the subject clear and remove distracting edges or borders. Do not stretch images to fit a space.
- **Honest editing only.** Brightness, contrast, straightening and cropping are fine. Nothing that changes what happened is: no added produce, no removed people, no altered conditions.
- **Text on photos** only sits on a calm area, or on a solid or strongly darkened overlay, so it stays readable.

**People and permission**

- Anyone shown up close gives permission first. Anyone featured prominently gives written consent.
- People are always shown with dignity, as skilled professionals and as people, never as objects of charity.
- Never publish a photo showing unsafe practice or a breach of the very standards we teach.

**Looking ahead.** When new projects are photographed, shoot in good daylight, keep the frame tidy, take a wide shot and a close detail at each stage, and keep the original full-resolution files. Messaging apps compress photos, so always send originals for anything that will be published.

## 11. Voice and messaging

We write the way an experienced consultant speaks to a client they respect: clearly, warmly, and without exaggeration. Facts persuade. Adjectives do not.

A good test: **if a sentence could appear in any company's brochure, rewrite it until it could only be Mekan's.**

### Generic vs Mekan

| Generic | Mekan |
|---|---|
| "We are a leading, world-class consultancy." | "Since 2018 we have helped Ethiopian farms and processors meet the standards their markets demand." |
| "Cutting-edge solutions for your business." | "We prepare your team, your records and your site for a GLOBALG.A.P audit." |
| "Guaranteed certification success." | "We work through every requirement with you before the auditor arrives." |
| "Eco-friendly, sustainable practices." | "Integrated pest management, soil and water planning, and records that prove it." |
| "Our expert team of professionals." | "Our trainers include agronomists and food-safety specialists with years in the field." |

### What we talk about

1. **Readiness.** What it takes to meet a standard, and how we get our clients there.
2. **Proof.** Partners, projects, certifications achieved and markets reached.
3. **Practice.** The real work: on the farm, in the pack house, in the training room.
4. **People.** Teams who are trained, confident and able to keep the standard every day.
5. **The long view.** Sustainable growth, resilient food systems, lasting relationships.

### How we sound

- **Plain.** Short sentences. Everyday words. Technical terms explained the first time.
- **Specific.** Name the standard, the crop, the market, the result.
- **Confident, not loud.** No exclamation marks in professional writing. No hype.
- **Warm.** We are talking to people, often under pressure. We are on their side.
- **Active.** "We train your team", not "Training is provided".

### Claims

Every statement about our experience, number of clients, certifications supported, partnerships or results must be something Mekan can stand behind and show if asked. **What we cannot prove, we do not say.** When exact numbers are not available, name the partners and the work instead of inventing figures.

**Role tokens.** Pages and components never use the raw colours above directly. They use role tokens (background, card, text, muted text, border, brand, accent, link) that point to the right brand colour in light mode and in dark mode. This is what makes dark mode correct by design rather than by patching.

| Role | Light mode | Dark mode |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Paper | Dark page |
| Card | White | Dark card |
| Border | Line | Dark line |

| Heading | Navy | Light text |
| Brand text | Navy | Sky |
| Accent text / links | Mekan Green | Sprout |

## 16. Looking after the brand

A brand stays strong when it is cared for in one place.

- **Brand owner.** Biniam Amberbir, Chief Executive Officer, approves anything new that carries the Mekan logo before it is printed or published.
- **Brand folder.** All official files live in one shared folder: logo files, this guide, fonts, templates (letterhead, proposal, report, presentation, certificate, email signature) and selected photographs sorted by project. Always take files from the folder, never from old emails or earlier documents.
- **The website.** Digital Aksumite maintains the website's design tokens and components in line with this guide.
- **Changes.** The logo, the palette and the typefaces change only by a management decision. Everything else in this guide can grow as Mekan grows.

Consistency does not mean everything looks the same. It means everything is unmistakably Mekan: steady, capable and ready for the world.

A door frame is only noticed when it fails. When it is set well, nobody thinks about it. People simply walk through.

A brand works the same way. Nobody will praise the colour of a heading or the space around a logo. What they will notice, without quite knowing why, is that everything from Mekan feels steady.
